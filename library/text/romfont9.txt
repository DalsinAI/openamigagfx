# OpenKick's ROM screen font, 9 pixels high: topaz.font 9 (10 x 9 cells,
# baseline row 7, one descender row). Drawn by hand, glyph by glyph, in
# this file for OpenGfx (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 AmigaChrome Team), as a
# size of its own (not romfont8.txt scaled). It is not traced or converted
# from any other font.
#
# The design is romfont8.txt's at this size: ink in columns 1-8 (columns 0
# and 9 are the gap between letters), so a two-pixel stem stands in the
# middle of a cell; vertical strokes two pixels wide, horizontal strokes
# one pixel; bowls and arches are rectangles with their corners cut by one
# pixel; capitals and digits rows 0-7; small letters rows 2-7 with
# ascenders to row 0; the bowls of g, p, q and y stand one row higher so
# that their tails fit rows 7-8. The zero is dotted, the slashed O is O.
#
# "@ c" starts a glyph (c a character, or 0xNN); "@ default" is the glyph
# for characters outside 32..255 and for 127..159. "@ accent.NAME" are the
# marks and "@ cap.X" the short capitals (rows 2-7) mkfont.py builds the
# accented Latin-1 letters from. Rows: '#' ink, '.' none.
