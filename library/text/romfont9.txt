# OpenKick's ROM screen font, 9 pixels high: topaz.font 9 (10 x 9 cells,
# baseline row 7, one descender row), the name old programs open. The letters
# are DejaVu Sans Mono's (see DEJAVU-LICENCE.txt), finished by hand in
# romfont8.txt and grown here a row taller and a column or two wider, each
# time repeating a row or column whose copy thickens no stroke, then finished
# by hand again, glyph by glyph (24 October 2026). It replaces the Team's
# earlier two-pixel-stem drawing; nothing in it comes from Commodore's or
# AROS's topaz.
#
# The design: one-pixel strokes only; ink in columns 1-7 (columns 8, 9 and 0
# are the gap); capitals, digits and ascenders rows 0-7; small letters rows
# 2-7; a tail takes row 8. The zero is dotted.
#
# "@ c" starts a glyph (c a character, or 0xNN); "@ default" is the glyph
# for characters outside 32..255 and for 127..159. "@ accent.NAME" are the
# marks and "@ cap.X" the short capitals (rows 2-7) mkfont.py builds the
# accented Latin-1 letters from. Rows: '#' ink, '.' none.
