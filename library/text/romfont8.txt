# OpenKick's ROM screen font, 8 pixels high: topaz.font 8 (8 x 8 cells,
# baseline row 6, one descender row). Drawn by hand, glyph by glyph, in
# this file for OpenGfx (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 AmigaChrome Team). It is
# not traced or converted from any other font.
#
# The design: ink in columns 0-6 (column 7 is the gap between letters);
# vertical strokes two pixels wide (a hires pixel on a 640 x 256 PAL screen
# is tall and narrow, and one-pixel stems fade there), horizontal strokes one
# pixel; bowls and arches are rectangles with their corners cut by one
# pixel; capitals and digits rows 0-6; small letters rows 2-6 with
# ascenders to row 0; the bowls of g, p, q and y stand one row higher so
# that their tails fit rows 6-7. The zero is dotted, the slashed O is O.
#
# "@ c" starts a glyph (c a character, or 0xNN); "@ default" is the glyph
# for characters outside 32..255 and for 127..159. "@ accent.NAME" are the
# marks and "@ cap.X" the short capitals (rows 2-6) mkfont.py builds the
# accented Latin-1 letters from. Rows: '#' ink, '.' none.
